Two minutes to your first sentence.

    Download

    Open Utter.dmg and drag the app to Applications. It’s still called Utter inside while the rename finishes.

  2. 2

    Allow it once

    Blabbit isn’t notarised (that needs a paid Apple account). In System Settings → Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway. Updates never ask again.

  3. 3

    Two permissions

    Microphone to hear you, Accessibility to type at your cursor.

  4. 4

    Talk

    Download the recommended voice model, Parakeet V2, when the app offers it. Then click into any text field and hold ⌥ Space.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Blabbit is open source under the MIT licence, with no paid tier, account or subscription. Model licences are shown in the Model Manager; most are MIT or Apache-2.0.

Is it listening all the time?

No. Blabbit uses the microphone only while you hold the shortcut, and a small overlay shows when it’s listening. The audio is transcribed on your Mac and never uploaded.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. Install takes about two minutes and the app walks you through it. After that there is one thing to remember: hold ⌥ Space, talk, let go. If you get stuck, ask on GitHub and you’ll get an answer from a person.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once a model is downloaded, dictation needs no connection. Blabbit goes online only to download a model you choose (checked with SHA-256), to check for updates (verified with an EdDSA signature), and for Anthropic rewriting if you opt in and turn off Local-only mode, which is on by default.

Which Macs does it run on?

Apple Silicon Macs (M1 or later) on macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Intel Macs aren’t supported.

Which languages can I dictate in?

Parakeet V2 is English only. For other languages, choose Parakeet V3 or a Whisper model in Settings → Models. The app itself is in English.

Why does macOS say it can’t verify Blabbit?

Notarisation needs a paid Apple developer account, which this free project doesn’t have. The app is signed and its source is public. Approve it once with Open Anyway; updates don’t ask again.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit Blabbit, delete it from Applications, and remove ~/Library/Application Support/Utter. The README lists every file Blabbit keeps.
